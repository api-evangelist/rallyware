# Rallyware

> Rallyware is an AI-powered sales performance and workforce enablement platform that guides distributed sellers, distributors, and field teams toward the next best action using real-time performance data, learning, gamification, and behavioral insights. Rallyware exposes open APIs, webhooks, and pre-built connectors to integrate with CRM, POS, WFM, inventory, HR, and enterprise systems, but its API is delivered to enterprise customers and is not published as a public developer portal.
